ARENARCH

The fight is all there is.

A free, fast team arena game for fans of classic MMO PvP who hate the grind. 1v1 duels and 3v3 team fights with respawns and no healers, everyone equal from minute one. Pick a class and a weapon, save your trump cards and outplay the enemy team.

Remember why you queued for arena? Not for the levels, not for the gear. For the fight. In ARENARCH, the fight is all there is.

No healers

No endless healing. Defensives, crowd control and timing decide everything. Die, respawn in seconds. The first team to reach the kill goal wins.

Everyone is equal

No levels. No stat gear. Equipment changes only how you look. You win by playing better, not by playing longer.

Classes and trump cards

8 classes, each with three weapon styles, talents and trump cards: powerful long cooldowns that persist through death. Burn one at the wrong moment and your team will feel it.

Addons built in

Damage meter, crowd control tracker, enemy cooldowns, cast bars, arena frames and distance to target. All there from the start, nothing to install.

Fast matches

Short, intense rounds, with a break between rounds to swap weapons and talents to counter your opponents.

Arena-wide ultimates

Every class has one ultimate every two minutes. It changes the whole arena, and each one has its own way to survive. It is a show even for those watching.

Who steps into the arena

Each people brings two classes. More about the world and its heroes on the Lore page.

Orcs

Red-skinned giants of the red canyons. To an orc the arena is sacred: here you rise in rank and settle the feuds of the clans.

Lean, swift wardens of the sacred groves. They hear the spirits of the forest and draw power from the turquoise crystals in the rocks.

Our promise

Free. Forever.

I'm an indie developer and a longtime arena player. ARENARCH is the arena I always wanted: a place to get friends together and run your own tournament, and a place to compete for real, at esports level. So the rules are simple.

Always free

Download and play for free. That stays true for good, not just at launch.

Looks only

Money buys only how your hero and weapons look. No power for money: in the arena skill wins, not your wallet.

No pressure

No aggressive marketing: no pop-ups, no "buy now" timers, no loot boxes.

Everything you buy goes to servers, new arenas and tournaments.
