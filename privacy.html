Privacy

Cookies and site statistics

What we collect

Only if you press "Accept", the site loads Yandex Metrica. It sets cookies and collects anonymous visit data: the page you came from, the pages you viewed and for how long, what you clicked, your device and browser type, and an approximate region from your IP address. Metrica's Session Replay records mouse movement and scrolling, but not what you type into fields.

Why

To understand how many people are interested in the game, where they hear about it, and what on the site is unclear. The data is not sold and not used for advertising.

Who processes the data

The statistics are processed by Yandex under its own rules: the Yandex privacy policy and the Yandex Metrica terms. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages.

The survey

Survey answers go to the developer through Google Forms (processed by Google under its privacy policy). Leaving a Discord or Steam name is optional: it is only used to invite you to the beta.

How to opt out

Press "Decline" in the strip at the bottom of the site and Metrica is never loaded. Changed your mind? The button below asks again. You can also delete cookies in your browser settings.

Contact

Questions about your data: ask in our Discord.
